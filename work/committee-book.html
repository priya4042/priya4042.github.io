Committee Book: getting the money maths of a BC exactly right

Turning the notebook that runs a savings group into an app - with bidding chits, partial payments and members who can check their own status.

Platform
Android
Built with
Flutter, Supabase
Status
Signed release, ready for Google Play

The problem

Millions of families and offices run committees - BCs, chits, kitties - on paper and WhatsApp. When a payment is disputed, there is no record everyone trusts. I couldn't find a free app that handled the way these groups actually work, so I built one.

What I built

  • Every way the pot is shared: fixed turn order, lucky draw, and bidding chits where the winner's discount is shared back with the others.
  • Real-life payments: partial payments, late fines, the organiser's commission, and a "cash in hand" figure that is always correct.
  • Members can check for themselves. The organiser can share a committee live. Joining needs the organiser's approval - a share code alone shows nothing - and the database only lets approved members read it.
  • Reminders and statements: WhatsApp, SMS and UPI payment links, PDF statements, and Hindi throughout.

The hard part

Money bugs are the worst kind of bug. In a bidding chit, what each member pays changes every cycle, so every calculation in the app goes through one function that knows the amount due for that cycle. Automated tests check the maths for fixed, partial, commission and bidding committees before every build - for example, in a four-person committee of 1,000 each, a winning bid of 1,000 with 5% commission must give each member exactly 200 back.

Where it is now

Signed release built and waiting for its Google Play listing.
