Tuition Book privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Tuition Book is made by Priya Tech Labs for tutors and coaching teachers. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

What stays on your phone

Batches, students' names, classes, photos and dates of birth, parents' names and phone numbers, fees, discounts, payments, attendance, test marks, notes from parent meetings, homework, planned tests, holidays, online class links, study material files, and your name and UPI ID are stored only on your phone. A student photo is taken with the camera or picked from the gallery only when you choose to add one. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload any of it.

When you send a reminder, a receipt, an absence note or a homework or test notice, the text is handed to WhatsApp, the SMS app or the app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send for that parent. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Study material and class links

If you add study material, you pick the file (a PDF, picture or document) with Android's file picker. The app copies only that file into its own folder on your phone; it needs no storage permission and uploads nothing. A file is shared only when you tap Share and pick an app. An online class link (such as Google Meet or Zoom) is saved with the batch; tapping "Join class" opens it in that app or your browser, and the link goes to parents only when you send it.

Importing students

If you import a student list, you pick a CSV or Excel file with Android's file picker. The app reads only that file, on your phone, and adds the students you confirm. It needs no storage permission and does not upload the file. The sample file is shared through Android's share sheet only when you ask for it.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. They contain your students' details, so keep them somewhere safe. Student photos are not part of the backup file. Study material files are included when they add up to 20 MB or less; above that, the backup lists them but does not contain them. If you choose "Save backup to Google Drive or a file", Android's own save screen opens and you pick where the file goes, such as your Google Drive. The app does not sign in to Google and does not read anything else in your Drive.

Receipts, reminders and report cards can be shared as a picture or a PDF. The file is made on your phone and goes only to the app you pick in the share sheet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, the app asks your phone to check your fingerprint, face or screen PIN. The phone does the check and tells the app only whether it passed. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Tuition Book widget to your home screen, it shows how many classes you have today and the total fees pending. Anyone who can see your home screen can see these two numbers. Remove the widget if you do not want that.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder (attendance, fee day, month-end reports, the evening before a test or before each class), the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults (tutors) and is not directed at children under 13. Students' details are entered by the tutor and stay on the tutor's phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
