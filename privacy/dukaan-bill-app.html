Dukaan Bill privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Dukaan Bill is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your shop profile (name, address, phone, GSTIN, UPI ID and logo), items and stock, bills, returns, customers and udhaar khata entries, and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them. Android's own cloud backup of the app's data is switched off, so your shop's records do not leave the phone unless you send them yourself.

Customers' details

The names and phone numbers you write in the khata or on a bill are kept on your phone only and used only when you choose: to put them on a bill or statement, to open WhatsApp with a reminder you can read before sending, or to open the phone dialler. The app does not read your contacts and has no SMS permission.

Files you share or save

Bills, statements, Excel and CSV files and backup files are made on the phone. They leave it only when you share them (through Android's share screen, to the app you pick) or save them to a place you pick (a folder, a pen drive or Google Drive). Printing goes through Android's print screen to the printer you choose. A backup file contains all your shop's records, so keep it somewhere safe.

Photos

A shop logo is chosen with Android's photo picker. Only the one picture you choose is read, made small and kept inside the app. No storage or camera permission is used.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on the home and reports pages only. There are no ads on the billing, payment, bill, item or khata pages, and no full-screen ads. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. Your bills, customers and stock are never given to AdMob. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

Children

Dukaan Bill is a business tool for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything it kept. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.
