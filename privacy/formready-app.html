FormReady privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

FormReady ("the app") is developed by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your photos and documents

Pictures you take or choose, and the JPG and PDF files FormReady makes from them, are processed only on your phone. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Files you save go to your phone's gallery; files you share go only where you send them.

Permissions

FormReady uses the Android photo picker and camera screen so you can choose or take a picture. It does not read the rest of your gallery. On Android 9 and older it asks for storage access only to save the finished file to your gallery.

Google ML Kit (on your phone)

Background removal for photos uses Google ML Kit selfie segmentation, and the document scanner uses Google ML Kit's scanner from Google Play services. Both run on your phone and your pictures are not sent to Google. Google Play services may download the scanner module and may send Google basic diagnostics and usage information about these features (for example, crash and performance data), as described in Google's ML Kit terms.

Exam alerts

To show admit card, result and notice alerts, the app downloads one data file from our website (https://priya4042.github.io/formready/data/app.json). It is fetched when you open the app and, through Android's WorkManager, about every 12 hours in the background when you are online, so you can be notified about the exams you follow. Nothing about you is sent with this request; like any web request, the website host (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address. The exams you follow are stored only on your phone. You can stop alerts by unfollowing the exams or turning off the app's notifications.

Reminders

Form deadline and exam day reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Stored on your phone

Your language, theme, last chosen exam, followed exams, reminders and tracker entries, so the app opens the same way next time.

Backup

If you use "Back up my data", the app makes a backup file of these settings and opens your phone's share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it; we never receive it. Restoring reads a backup file you pick.

Advertising

The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. You can also choose to watch a rewarded video ad to turn banners off for a day; it is shown only when you tap to watch it. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic usage data. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses data from apps that show its ads.

Not a government app

FormReady is not affiliated with any exam body or government. Photo sizes, age limits and exam dates follow official notices, which can change; the official sources are listed in the app under About > Sources and disclaimer.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
