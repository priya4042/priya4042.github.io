Pehchano India privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Pehchano India is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your quiz records (quizzes finished, right answers, best scores, streaks, the Daily 10 ticks), your hints, your badges, the Learn cards you opened, the list of recently asked questions and your choices (app language, question language, look, sound, vibration and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All questions, facts and pictures are inside the app, so it works without internet. The app has no map and downloads no map data.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permission. It has no access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files or messages. The sounds are made by the app on your phone; vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only; now and then a full-screen ad after a finished quiz's result is closed (never during a quiz, not on the first open, at most once every three finished quizzes and six minutes apart); and a video you can choose to watch on the home page for extra hints. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is made for students and families. It is not directed at children under 13; a child should use it with a parent.

Links to official pages

Each fact links to the Government of India page it comes from. Opening one starts your browser; the links carry no tracking parameters. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing. It sends no notifications.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
