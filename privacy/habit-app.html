Habit Streak privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Habit Streak (Roz ki Aadat) is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter (your habits, their days and reminder times, ticks, counts, water amounts, moods and day notes) is stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload it.

The month review card and the year view are pictures made on your phone. They leave the phone only when you share them, through Android's share screen, to the app you choose. What happens next is up to you and that app.

CSV export

"Save ticks as CSV" and "Send ticks as CSV file" make a file on your phone with the date, habit name, value and unit of every tick. It goes only where you choose, through Android's own save or share screen. The app uploads nothing.

Backups

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. Keep them somewhere safe. If you choose "Save backup to Google Drive or a folder", Android's own save screen opens and the file goes only where you pick (for example your Google Drive, under Google's policy). The app does not sign in to Drive or read anything else there. Restoring works the same way: you pick the file.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, Habit Streak asks your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) to confirm it is you. Android checks this and tells the app only whether it passed. Habit Streak never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the "use biometric hardware" permission.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Habit Streak widget to your home screen, it shows today's progress and the names of up to four of today's habits, with tick buttons. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it, and tap its buttons. The widget's data is kept on your phone only. Remove the widget to stop this.

Notifications

If you set a reminder for a habit, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. You can turn a reminder off at any time in the habit's settings.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Health

Habit Streak is a motivation tool. It does not give health, fitness or medical advice, and it does not read any health data from your phone. Targets such as glasses of water are only what you choose to track.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
