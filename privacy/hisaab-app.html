Hisaab privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Hisaab ("the app") is developed by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your numbers

Everything you type into Hisaab is calculated on your phone. It is not uploaded, stored on a server or seen by us. When you share a result, it goes only where you send it.

Stored on your phone

Your language and theme choice.

Not financial advice

Results are estimates for planning. Check final figures with your bank, employer or a tax professional.

Advertising

The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic usage data. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses data from apps that show its ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
