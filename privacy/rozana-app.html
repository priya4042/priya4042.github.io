Rozana privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Rozana is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter (items, quantities, rates, payments, seller names and phone numbers, and in seller mode your customers' names, mobile numbers and addresses) is stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload it.

Your UPI ID, if you add it, is used only on your phone to draw a pay QR code and a UPI pay link on bills, statements and reminders. Nothing about it goes to us or to any server; the person paying opens it in their own UPI app. Purchase costs, your own expenses (fuel, cattle feed, wages and so on), profit figures and the price list card in seller mode also stay on your phone; the price list picture leaves the phone only when you share it.

When you send a bill, statement (PDF or picture) or payment reminder, the text, picture or file is handed to WhatsApp, your SMS app or the app you choose through Android's share screen. The app never sends an SMS by itself; you press send in your SMS app. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Voice entry

If you tap the mic, the app opens your phone's own speech recogniser (for example Google's). That recogniser records your voice and turns it into text under its own privacy policy, and may use the internet to do so. Rozana receives only the text, shows you what it understood and saves nothing until you confirm. Rozana does not record or keep any audio and has no microphone permission.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. Keep them somewhere safe. If you choose "Save backup to Google Drive or a folder", Android's own save screen opens and the file goes only where you pick (for example your Google Drive, under Google's policy). The app does not sign in to Drive or read anything else there. Restoring works the same way: you pick the file.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, Rozana asks your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) to confirm it is you. Android checks this and tells the app only whether it passed. Rozana never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the "use biometric hardware" permission.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Rozana widget to your home screen, it shows today's amount and quantities (or, in seller mode, how many customers got their delivery today). Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. The numbers are kept on your phone only. Remove the widget to stop this.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
