Vehicle Book privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Vehicle Book is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your vehicles and their number plates, fuel fills, services, tyre and battery changes, expenses, trips, vehicle loan details and EMIs, and the papers, expiry dates and paper photos you enter are stored only on your phone. A photo is taken with the camera or picked from the gallery only when you choose to add one. The app has no account and no server, and it does not connect to any government service. We never see, collect or upload your data.

Scanning a fuel bill

When you tap Scan bill, you take a photo of the bill or pick one from your gallery. The text is read on your phone by Google ML Kit text recognition, using a model that ships inside the app, so the photo and its text are not sent anywhere. Only the numbers you save are kept; the app does not keep the bill photo. Like all ML Kit features, it may send Google diagnostic and usage information such as the device model, OS version, app version, a per-installation identifier, how long recognition took and error codes, over an encrypted connection. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. Keep them somewhere safe. Paper photos are not part of the backup file.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

Notifications

When you add a paper or a loan, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules reminders on your phone: before a paper expires, a day before each EMI, and a gentle note when tyres or the battery pass the limits you set. It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. You can turn reminders off from the menu at any time.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
