Dimaag Daily privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Dimaag Daily is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your daily Brain Scores, session scores, the level each game has reached, your streak and streak freezes, and your choices (language, sound, vibration, colour patterns and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Every daily set and every puzzle is made inside the app from the date or a number, so the app works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Badges and the home-screen widget

Badges you earn are worked out from your scores and stored only on your phone. If you add the Dimaag Daily widget to your home screen, it shows today's workout status and your streak from the same data on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the widget shows no ads.

Daily reminder

The daily reminder is off until you turn it on in Settings. When you do, Android asks you to allow notifications, and the app schedules one notification a day on your phone at the time you pick. No exact-alarm permission is used and nothing is sent anywhere. Turn it off in Settings at any time.

Extra question packs

When the phone is online, at most once a day the app downloads a small list of extra question packs, and any new pack, from priya4042.github.io. The packs are plain data (sums and number runs with their names in eight languages), checked strictly on your phone before use, and never contain code. Nothing about you is sent. The app works fully without them.

Brain age

The brain age shown in the app is a playful number worked out from your score. It is not a medical test and says nothing about your health.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share on the result card, the app makes a picture of the card and a short text (the day number, your Brain Score, brain age, skill dots and streak, never which games were played) and hands them to the app you choose through Android's share screen. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad after a finished workout or practice (never during a game, not in your first session, at most once every three sessions). You can choose to watch a rewarded video to get a streak freeze; this is optional. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads, the version check and the pack download above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
