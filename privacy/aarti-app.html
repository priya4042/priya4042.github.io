Aarti Sangrah privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Aarti Sangrah is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your favourite texts, bookmarked verses, reading (path) counts and goals, mala jaap counts by day, the mantra you chose for jaap, the last text you read, the days you read or counted jaap (for the reading streak), your puja samagri checklists (ticks, items you added or removed, and lists you made), your festival reminders (name, date and time), your reminder times and your reading choices (text size, night page, English letters, auto-scroll speed) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All the aartis, chalisas, mantras and stotras are inside the app and work without internet.

When you share a verse as a picture, copy a verse or share a samagri list as text (including "Send on WhatsApp"), the picture or text is handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen or clipboard. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself, either through the share screen or through Android's own save screen, where you can pick Google Drive or any folder. The app has no Google sign-in and does not upload anything itself.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice (on most phones this is provided by Google). The verse text is handed to that voice on your phone to be spoken. The app does not record audio and has no microphone permission.

Keeping the screen on and vibration

While a text or the jaap counter is open, the app can ask Android to keep the screen awake; this needs no permission and stops when you leave the page. The jaap counter uses the phone's touch feedback, which follows your phone's own vibration setting.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, the app asks Android to check your phone's own fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. Android does the check and only tells the app whether it passed; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the biometric permission.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Aarti Sangrah widget to your home screen, it shows today's aarti and today's jaap count. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. Remove the widget if you do not want that.

Reminders

If you turn on the morning or evening puja reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone, one for each day of the week, naming that day's aarti (on Sunday, the Surya mantras). It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings.

Festival reminders are ones you set yourself, with a name, date and time, once or every year. The app has no festival dates of its own. They are scheduled on your phone with the same notification permission and can be deleted at any time.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page and the "Panchang app" card on the festival reminders page open our website in your browser. The "Sources of this list" links on a samagri list open the published page the list was gathered from. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, only on list pages. No ads are shown while a text is open for reading or on the jaap counter. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
