Number Rain privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Number Rain is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The player names you type, each player's choices (target, sums, speed, big numbers, colour-safe colours), best scores, stats, badges, daily rain results and streak, and the app choices (language, digits, sounds, vibration and motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Player names are only shown inside the app so a family can share one phone. Use a first name or a nickname; the app never sends it anywhere.

Every rain, including the daily rain, is made inside the app from a number or the date, so the game works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The soft rain and chimes are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Sharing your daily result

When you tap Share on the daily rain card, the app makes a picture of the card and a short line ("Daily rain #N: points, pairs") and hands them to the app you choose through Android's share screen. What happens next is up to you and that app. The card carries no player name.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad after a game has ended (never while rain is falling, at most once every 3 games, and not on the first open). If the pond overflows in Classic, you can choose to watch a rewarded video to continue that game once. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

Number Rain has a Learn mode that helps children practise number bonds, and the game is meant to be played by families. Where Google Play lists the app for children, its ads follow Google Play's Families rules: only ads suitable for children, no personalised ads for children, and no data from children used for ads. A grown-up can turn off ad personalisation in the phone's settings at any time. The app itself collects nothing from anyone.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
