Number Path privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Number Path is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The stars you earn on each level, your hints, the rules you have read about, your daily puzzle results and streak, your badges, the extra level packs you have downloaded and your stars on them, and your choices (language, sound, vibration, reduce motion and the daily reminder time) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Every level and every daily puzzle is made inside the app from a number or the date, so the app works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Daily reminder

The daily puzzle reminder is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications (Android 13 and newer) and schedules one local notification a day at the time you pick. The reminder is made and shown on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere. Turn it off in Settings, or block the app's notifications in the phone settings, at any time.

Extra level packs

At most once a day, when you are online, the app reads a small list called packs.json from priya4042.github.io/content/number-path/ and downloads any new level pack listed there. A pack is only data (board sizes, numbers, rules and a checked answer path, with names in the eight app languages); the app never runs code from it, and checks every pack strictly before keeping it. You can also check for packs from the Extra levels page. Nothing about you is sent, apart from the ordinary request your phone makes to fetch a file. Everything else in the app works without these packs and without internet.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share on the daily result, the app makes a picture of the result card and a short text (the puzzle number, your stars, time, hints used and streak; never the board, the numbers or your path) and hands them to the app you choose through Android's share screen. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad when you move on from a solved level (never while a board is being played, not on your first open, at most once every four solved levels and five minutes). When you run out of hints you can choose to watch a short rewarded video for one more hint; this is optional. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads, the version check and the level pack check above, the app collects nothing.

Browser version

Number Path can also be played in a web browser at priya4042.github.io. The browser version has no ads, no reminder and no version check; your progress is kept in your browser's own storage on that device, and it can read the same level packs from the same website.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
