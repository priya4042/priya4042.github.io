Neon Snake Arena privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Neon Snake Arena is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your best scores, arena stars, Daily Arena scores and streak, the skins and badges you have earned, the skins you picked, any extra arena packs and their stars, a few counters (how much food you have eaten, how many runs and duels you finished) and your choices (language, controls, sound, vibration, reduce motion, colour-blind-safe colours and the daily reminder setting) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Every game runs on your phone, so the app works without internet. The Daily Arena picks the same arena for everyone from the date alone; nothing is sent to do that.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Daily reminder

The Daily Arena reminder is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, your phone shows one notification a day at the time you picked. On Android 13 and newer the app asks for permission to show notifications when you turn it on; you can say no, and the app works the same. The reminder is set up on your phone only; nothing is sent anywhere. The app asks for no exact-alarm permission.

Extra arena packs

At most once a day, when you are online, the app downloads a small list of extra arena packs from priya4042.github.io and any new pack on it. These files hold only arena shapes and names; the app checks every one strictly before using it and never runs anything from them. Nothing about you is sent, beyond the ordinary web request any website sees. You never need these packs to play; the app works fully without internet.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only; now and then a full-screen ad between sessions, after a run or duel is finished and closed (never during a game, not on your first open, at most once every two sessions); and an optional video you can choose to watch to continue a run once. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

Neon Snake Arena is made for teens and young adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Light sensitivity

Nothing in the app flashes more than three times a second and the screen never strobes. If you are sensitive to light, turn on Reduce motion and stop playing if you feel unwell.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps, sharing and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters. "Share my score" and "Share with friends" hand a short text to the app you choose through Android's share screen.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads, the version check and the arena pack download above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://priya4042.github.io/privacy/neon-snake-app.html with a new date.
