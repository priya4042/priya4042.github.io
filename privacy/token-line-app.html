Token Line privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Token Line is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The name, logo and notice of your place, your services and counters, every token you give (with any name, phone number, note and priority you type), the call times, the reports and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them. Android's own cloud backup of the app's data is switched off, so your records do not leave the device unless you send them yourself.

Customers' details

A name, phone number or note on a token is optional. It is kept on the device only and used only when you choose: to print it on the slip, to show it on the call screen, or to open WhatsApp with the token message you can read before sending. The app does not read your contacts and has no SMS or call permission. A priority (elderly, pregnant, disability or emergency) is shown openly on the board so the waiting room can see why a token goes first; do not choose one unless the person agrees.

Spoken calls

Calls are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech voice, on the device. No recording is made and the microphone is never used. The sound plays through the phone speaker or a speaker you have connected yourself.

Display board

The board shows token numbers, counter names, priorities, the next five tokens and wait estimates. Names and phone numbers are never shown on the board. To show it on a TV you cast or mirror the screen yourself with Android's own feature or a cable; the app does not connect to any TV.

Admin PIN

If you set an admin PIN, only a salted, slow one-way hash of it is stored on the device. The PIN itself is never stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it, the only way back is to clear the app's data in Android settings, which also removes the tokens, so keep a backup.

Files you share or save

Token slips, reports (PDF, Excel, CSV) and backup files are made on the device. They leave it only when you share them (through Android's share screen, to the app you pick) or save them to a place you pick. Printing goes through Android's print screen to the printer you choose. A backup file contains all your records, so keep it somewhere safe.

Photos

A logo is chosen with Android's photo picker. Only the one picture you choose is read, made small and kept inside the app. No storage or camera permission is used.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on the home and reports pages only. There are no ads on the call screen, the display board, the token page or any form, and no full-screen ads. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. Your tokens, names and phone numbers are never given to AdMob. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

Children

Token Line is a tool for businesses and offices. It is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything it kept. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

ग्राहकों की जानकारी

टोकन पर नाम, फ़ोन नंबर या नोट अपनी मर्ज़ी से है। यह सिर्फ़ डिवाइस में रहता है और तभी काम आता है जब आप चुनें: पर्ची पर छापना, बुलाने की स्क्रीन पर दिखाना, या WhatsApp पर टोकन का संदेश खोलना जिसे भेजने से पहले आप पढ़ सकें। ऐप आपके संपर्क नहीं पढ़ता और उसके पास SMS या कॉल की अनुमति नहीं है। प्राथमिकता (बुज़ुर्ग, गर्भवती, दिव्यांग या इमरजेंसी) बोर्ड पर खुलकर दिखती है ताकि सबको पता रहे कि टोकन पहले क्यों गया; व्यक्ति की सहमति के बिना इसे न चुनें।

बोलकर बुलाना

पुकार आपके फ़ोन की अपनी टेक्स्ट-टू-स्पीच आवाज़ से, डिवाइस पर ही, बोली जाती है। कोई रिकॉर्डिंग नहीं होती और माइक्रोफ़ोन कभी इस्तेमाल नहीं होता। आवाज़ फ़ोन के स्पीकर या आपके जोड़े हुए स्पीकर से आती है।

डिस्प्ले बोर्ड

बोर्ड पर टोकन नंबर, काउंटर के नाम, प्राथमिकता, अगले पाँच टोकन और इंतज़ार का अनुमान दिखता है। नाम और फ़ोन नंबर बोर्ड पर कभी नहीं दिखते। TV पर दिखाने के लिए आप ख़ुद Android की सुविधा या केबल से स्क्रीन कास्ट या मिरर करते हैं; ऐप किसी TV से नहीं जुड़ता।

एडमिन PIN

अगर आप एडमिन PIN रखते हैं, तो डिवाइस में उसका सिर्फ़ नमक मिला, धीमा, एकतरफ़ा हैश रहता है। PIN ख़ुद न कहीं रखा जाता है, न भेजा जाता है। भूल जाएँ तो Android सेटिंग में ऐप का डेटा साफ़ करना ही रास्ता है, जिससे टोकन भी मिट जाते हैं, इसलिए बैकअप रखें।
