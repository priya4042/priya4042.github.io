Chaupar Ghar privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Chaupar Ghar is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your results by mode and level, captures and pieces brought home, a game left part way, and your choices (language, sound, vibration, reduce motion, large text, high contrast, move hints, auto-move, undo and house rules) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

The game, the computer player and the throws all run inside the app, so the app works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The shell, dice and wooden sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad after a finished game (never during a game, not on the first open, at most once every two finished games and five minutes). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

आपके फ़ोन पर क्या रहता है

हर तरह और स्तर के आपके नतीजे, मारी गई गोटियाँ, घर लाई गोटियाँ, बीच में छोड़ा गया खेल, और आपकी पसंद (भाषा, आवाज़, कंपन, कम हलचल, बड़े अक्षर, तेज़ रंग, चाल के संकेत, अपने आप चाल, चाल वापसी और घरेलू नियम) केवल आपके फ़ोन पर रहते हैं। ऐप में कोई अकाउंट या सर्वर नहीं है। हम इन्हें कभी नहीं देखते, न इकट्ठा करते हैं, न अपलोड करते हैं।

खेल, कंप्यूटर खिलाड़ी और फेंक सब ऐप के अंदर चलते हैं, इसलिए ऐप बिना इंटरनेट के चलता है।

अगर आपके फ़ोन में Google बैकअप चालू है, तो Android ऐप का डेटा आपके अपने Google अकाउंट के बैकअप में रख सकता है। वह बैकअप आपके Google अकाउंट का है; हम उसे कभी नहीं देखते।

आवाज़ और कंपन

कौड़ियों, पासों और लकड़ी की आवाज़ें ऐप आपके फ़ोन पर ही बनाता है। ऐप को माइक्रोफ़ोन की अनुमति नहीं है और वह कुछ रिकॉर्ड नहीं करता। कंपन फ़ोन का सामान्य टच फ़ीडबैक है और इसके लिए किसी अनुमति की ज़रूरत नहीं।
