Emoji Guess privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Emoji Guess is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your coins, the puzzles you have solved, friends' puzzles you have solved, your daily puzzle results and streak, your badges, and your choices (app language, puzzle language, sound, vibration, reduce motion and the reminder time) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All built-in puzzles are inside the app, so it works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Puzzles you make and share

When you make a puzzle, the app turns your emoji and answer into a short message that you send yourself, through any app you choose on the share screen. Nothing goes to us. When you paste a friend's message, the app reads the puzzle from it on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere. The clipboard is read only when you tap Paste.

Extra puzzle packs

At most once a day, when you are online, the app downloads a small list of extra puzzle packs from our website (priya4042.github.io/content/emoji-guess/) and any new pack on it. You can also look for new packs from Settings. These files hold only puzzle words and emoji; the app checks them strictly before using them and never runs anything from them. The request is an ordinary web download: nothing about you, your progress or your device is sent with it, and we keep no record of who downloaded what (GitHub, which hosts the website, may keep its usual server logs). Packs are kept on your phone, and the app works fully without them.

Daily reminder

The daily puzzle reminder is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, your phone shows one notification a day at the time you pick. It is scheduled on your phone by Android; nothing is sent to us. On Android 13 and newer, the app asks for permission to show notifications only when you turn the reminder on. Turn it off any time in Settings.

Playing in a browser

The browser version on our website shows no ads and has no reminder. Your progress there is saved in your own browser's storage on that device; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, now and then a full-screen ad when you move on after solving a puzzle (never while a puzzle is open, not on the first open, at most once every five solved puzzles and five minutes), and an optional video you can choose to watch for a free letter. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Coins

Coins are earned only by solving puzzles. The app sells nothing and has no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads, the version check and the puzzle pack downloads above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

रोज़ का रिमाइंडर

आज की पहेली का रिमाइंडर तब तक बंद रहता है जब तक आप उसे सेटिंग में चालू न करें। चालू होने पर आपका फ़ोन आपके चुने समय पर दिन में एक सूचना दिखाता है। इसे आपके फ़ोन पर Android तय करता है; हमें कुछ नहीं भेजा जाता। Android 13 और नए फ़ोन पर ऐप सूचना दिखाने की अनुमति सिर्फ़ तब माँगता है जब आप रिमाइंडर चालू करते हैं। इसे कभी भी सेटिंग में बंद करें।
