Vivah Biodata privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Vivah Biodata is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type into a biodata (names, dates, family and contact details) and every photo you add is stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload your biodata or photos.

When you tap Share, Save as Word file or Print, the PDF, picture or Word file is handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or a printer) through Android's share and print screens. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. They contain your biodatas and all their photos (main photo, kundli and extra photos); keep them somewhere safe.

When you choose Save backup to Google Drive or a folder, or Save JPG, Android's own save screen opens and the file goes only where you pick (for example your Google Drive or the Downloads folder). The app does not sign in to Google Drive and cannot read anything else there.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

Guna milan, QR code and widget

Guna milan works on the phone from the nakshatras you pick or that are already in your biodatas; nothing is sent anywhere. If you choose the contact QR code, the code printed on the biodata holds the contact person's name and phone number, so anyone who scans the biodata can save them. The home-screen widget shows how many biodatas you have and the name on the latest one; remove the widget if you do not want that on your home screen.

App lock

The optional app lock uses your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) through Android. The app only learns whether the unlock succeeded; it never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.

Photos and camera

The app asks for the camera or opens Android's photo picker only when you choose to add a photo. It does not read other photos or files.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
