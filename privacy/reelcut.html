ReelCut privacy policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

ReelCut ("the app") turns your own videos into short clips. Your privacy is simple: everything happens on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. ReelCut does not collect, upload, transmit, or share any personal information or any of your content. There is no account, no login, and no server.

  • Your videos are never uploaded anywhere. All cutting, cropping, captions, and effects are done on your phone.
  • The app has no internet permission - it cannot send your data off the device even in principle.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising IDs, trackers, or third-party data collection SDKs.

What the app accesses (only on your device)

  • Your photos/videos - so you can pick a video to edit and save the results back to your gallery. These stay on your device.
  • Camera - only if you choose to record a video in the app.

Where files go

Finished shorts are saved to your device's gallery when you tap Save. The app keeps your in-app preferences (aspect ratio, watermark text, etc.) on the device only.

Children

ReelCut is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
