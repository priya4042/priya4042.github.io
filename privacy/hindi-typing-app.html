Hindi Typing privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Hindi Typing is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

What you type in lessons and tests is used only to check it against the practice text, and is not saved or sent anywhere. Your chosen layout and your lesson and test results are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

If you share a result card, the app makes a picture of your result on the phone and hands it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp. The name on the card is optional; it is saved only on your phone so you do not have to type it again, and it leaves the phone only inside a picture you choose to share.

Keyboards

If you connect a USB or Bluetooth keyboard, the app reads its key presses only while a lesson or test is open, to check your typing. Nothing is recorded outside the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for typing tests and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
