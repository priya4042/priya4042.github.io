Hindi Typing privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Hindi Typing is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

What you type in lessons and tests is used only to check it against the practice text, and is not saved or sent anywhere. Your chosen layout and your lesson and test results are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. A backup holds your results, the weak-key counts, your typing game best scores and your settings. When you choose "Save backup to Google Drive or a file", Android's own save screen opens and the file goes only where you pick, such as your Google Drive or a folder on the phone. The app does not sign in to Google Drive or read anything else there.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

If you share a result card, the app makes a picture of your result on the phone and hands it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp. The name on the card is optional; it is saved only on your phone so you do not have to type it again, and it leaves the phone only inside a picture you choose to share.

If you make a practice certificate, the app draws it on the phone from your best test result saved in the app and the name you type, and turns it into a PDF. The PDF leaves the phone only when you share it to an app you choose, or when you print it through Android's print service and the printer or print app you pick. The name is saved only on your phone, the same as the name on the result card.

Typing game scores (your best score for each layout) are stored only on your phone and are included in backups you make.

App lock, reminder and home-screen widget

The app lock is off unless you turn it on. It uses your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) through Android; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This needs the "use biometric hardware" permission.

The daily practice reminder is off unless you turn it on. It is a notification set up on the phone itself; nothing is sent to a server. It needs the notification permission, and the "run at startup" permission so the reminder still comes after the phone restarts.

If you add the home-screen widget, it shows your days-in-a-row streak and best test speed on your home screen. It reads them from the app on the phone.

Keyboards

If you connect a USB or Bluetooth keyboard, the app reads its key presses only while a lesson or test is open, to check your typing. Nothing is recorded outside the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for typing tests and is not directed at children under 13.
