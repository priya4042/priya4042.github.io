Zameen Napai privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Zameen Napai is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The measurements you enter, the plots you save with their notes, photos and the way their front faces, your chosen state, your app language and any unit values you set for your area are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Construction cost estimates are worked out on your phone and are not saved. The More apps screen opens our website in your browser, with no tracking added to the link.

When you tap Share, the text, picture or PDF is made on your phone and handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen. A plot drawing shows side lengths and the shape, not the GPS positions of the corners. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself, including the photos of your plots. You can share a backup, or save it through Android's own save screen to a folder or to your Google Drive; the app does not sign in to Google and never reads your Drive. Keep backups somewhere safe.

Photos

You can add photos to a saved plot. You take them with your phone's camera app or pick them from your gallery through Android's own picker, so the app needs no camera or storage permission. A copy is kept inside the app's private storage and is never uploaded.

App lock and home-screen widget

If you turn on App lock, the app asks Android to check your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern). The app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.

If you add the home-screen widget, it shows the name and area of your last saved plot on your home screen, where anyone who can see your phone can read it.

Land records links

The app lists links to state government land-records websites. They open in your browser; what you do on those sites is covered by their own policies. The app is not linked to any government office.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

Location

You pick your state yourself; the app does not use your location for that.

If you choose to measure a plot by walking, the app asks for location permission and reads GPS only while that screen is open. The corners you mark are used on your phone to work out the area. If you save the plot, its corner positions are stored only on your phone, inside the app. Your location is never uploaded or shared by the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Not an official record

Unit values are the usual ones for each state and can differ by district. They are for reference only. Confirm with your local revenue office or your sale deed before buying or selling land. Stamp duty and registration rates are built into the app with the date they were checked; rates change, so confirm them with your sub-registrar.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
