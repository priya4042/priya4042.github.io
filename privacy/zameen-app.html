Zameen Napai privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Zameen Napai is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The measurements you enter, the plots you save, your chosen state and any unit values you set for your area are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

When you tap Share, the text is handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. Keep them somewhere safe.

Location

The app does not use your location. You pick your state yourself.

Not an official record

Unit values are the usual ones for each state and can differ by district. They are for reference only. Confirm with your local revenue office or your sale deed before buying or selling land.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
