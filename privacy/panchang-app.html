Panchang privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Panchang ("the app") is developed by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles.

No personal data

Panchang works everything out on your phone. It does not ask for your location, contacts or any account.

Stored on your phone

The city you pick, your language and your theme.

Accuracy

Times are calculated for the chosen city. Local traditions can keep a festival a day apart; follow your temple or family tradition for rituals.

Advertising

The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic usage data. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses data from apps that show its ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
