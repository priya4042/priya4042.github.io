Panchang privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Panchang ("the app") is developed by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles.

No account, no personal data sent to us

Panchang works out tithi, nakshatra, sunrise and festival dates on your phone. It has no sign-in and does not ask for your contacts.

Stored on your phone

The city you pick, your language, your theme, your reminders and your own dates.

Location (optional)

When you choose a city, you can tap "Use my location". Only then does the app ask for your approximate (coarse) location. It reads it once, on the phone, to set your place and time zone, and saves only that place on your phone. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is never read in the background. If you do not allow it, pick a city from the list instead.

Internet

The app downloads a small rules file from our website (https://priya4042.github.io/panchang/data/rules.json) with festival date corrections and state holiday lists. Nothing about you is sent with it; like any web request, the website host (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address. Links you tap, such as official holiday sources, open in your browser.

Read aloud

The aarti read-aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google speech services). The app passes the aarti text to that engine on your phone; the engine works under its own maker's privacy policy.

Notifications

Festival and vrat reminders and the daily alert are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Accuracy

Times are calculated for the chosen city. Local traditions can keep a festival a day apart; follow your temple or family tradition for rituals. Panchang is not a government app; holiday lists are a guide only, so check the official sources listed in the app.

Advertising

The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic usage data. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses data from apps that show its ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
