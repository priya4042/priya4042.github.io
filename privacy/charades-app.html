Charades Night privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Charades Night is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Team names and colours, the cards you add yourself, your records (games played, cards guessed, best turn), the list of recently played cards and your choices (app language, card language, sound, vibration and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

The badges you earn are stored only on your phone as well.

The built-in cards are part of the app, so it works without internet.

Extra card packs from our website

When the phone is online, at most once a day (or when you tap New card packs in Settings), the app downloads a small list of card packs from priya4042.github.io and any new pack on it. These files hold only card words; nothing in them runs as code, and each pack is checked before it is used. The request is an ordinary web request, like opening a page, and carries nothing about you or your games. Downloaded packs are kept on your phone so they work offline. GitHub, which hosts the website, may keep standard web server logs such as IP addresses.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds, vibration, motion sensor and screen

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback. In Forehead mode the app reads the phone's motion sensor only during a turn, to notice when you tilt the phone; the readings are used at once and never stored or sent. During a game the screen is kept awake so it does not lock mid-turn.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad between games (never during a game, not in your first session, at most once every two finished games, three a day and ten minutes apart). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is a family party game meant for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13; younger children should play with an adult holding the phone.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Screen readers

When a screen reader such as TalkBack is on, the app tells it about turns, time left and scores. This uses the phone's own accessibility service; nothing is recorded or sent.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads, the version check and the card pack download above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
