Dawai & Sehat privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Dawai & Sehat is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The family members, medicines (names, doses, times, food timing, dates, days of the week, notes, stock counts), the record of doses taken, skipped or missed, health readings (blood pressure, pulse, blood sugar, weight and temperature, with notes), doctors (names, speciality, phone numbers and clinics), doctor visits, children's birth dates and vaccine records (which vaccines were given or not needed, the dates and your notes, and any vaccines you add by hand), the list of medicines to buy and photos of medicine strips that you enter are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Not medical advice

The app only reminds you and keeps your notes. It does not give medical advice, does not diagnose and does not suggest doses. Reminders can be delayed or missed if the phone's battery settings stop apps or the phone is off. Always follow your doctor.

Sharing, reports and printing

When you share today's dose status on WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp (or Android's share screen) with the message filled in; you choose who to send it to and press send yourself. When you share the list of medicines to buy, it goes the same way, as text or a picture with only the medicine names, the strength or dose as written and the quantity. When you share a report for the doctor or a dose history report as a PDF or a picture, the file is handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen. When you print a report, it goes to Android's own print screen. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Backups

Backups are files that you create and save yourself, either through the share screen or through Android's own save screen, where you can pick Google Drive or any folder. The app has no Google sign-in and does not upload anything itself. Backups contain your family's medicines, the list of medicines to buy, dose history, health readings, doctors, visits and children's vaccine records, so keep them somewhere safe. When you add a family member's backup to yours, it is read on your phone and merged there. Strip photos are not included in backup files; they stay on the phone and are deleted when you delete the medicine or uninstall the app.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

Strip photos

When you add a photo of a medicine strip, the app opens your phone's own camera app or photo picker. Only the one picture you take or choose is copied into the app's private folder on your phone. The app has no camera or storage permission and never uploads photos.

Calling a doctor

The call button opens your phone's dialer with the doctor's number. You place the call yourself; the app does not call or read anything.

Reminders

The app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone for each dose time, for refills when the stock runs low, before doctor visits and before a child's vaccine is due. If you allow "Alarms and reminders", they come at the exact minute; otherwise Android may bring them a few minutes late. They are set again after the phone restarts. The Taken, Skip and Later buttons on a reminder are handled on your phone.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, the app asks Android to check your phone's own fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. Android does the check and only tells the app whether it passed; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the biometric permission.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Dawai & Sehat widget to your home screen, it shows the next dose: the family member's name, the medicine, the dose and the time. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. Remove the widget if you do not want that.

More apps

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page opens our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. Your medicines, readings and other health notes are never given to AdMob. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults, who may use it to manage medicines for their children. It is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
