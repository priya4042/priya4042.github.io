Pass the Phone Quiz privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Pass the Phone Quiz is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The player names and faces you type in for a party game, your last game settings (rounds, categories, difficulty and timer), the list of recently asked questions (so the next games avoid repeats), your best solo streak, your Daily 10 result and streak, and your choices (language, question language, sound and vibration) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All questions ship inside the app and the Daily 10 is picked inside the app from the date, so the game works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing.

Sharing your Daily 10 result

When you tap Share on the Daily 10 card, the app makes a picture of the card and a short line ("Daily 10 #N: R/10 right, P points") and hands them to the app you choose through Android's share screen. The card never shows the questions or answers.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page, and now and then a full-screen ad between two games (never during a game, and not in your first session). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The questions are family friendly, but the app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
