Yojana Finder privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Yojana Finder is made by Priya Tech Labs. It is not a government app and is not affiliated with any government department. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your answers to the questions (state, age, gender, area, work, income range, social category, ration or BPL card, disability and family situation), your saved schemes and the documents you tick as ready are stored only on your phone. They are used only to sort schemes for you. The app has no account and no server, and we never see, collect or upload your answers.

We never ask for Aadhaar, bank details, OTP or any payment.

Reminders

When you set a reminder on a scheme, the date and your note are stored only on your phone, and the app shows a notification at 9 AM that day. Android asks you once to allow notifications; the app uses them only for the reminders you set. The app also re-sets your reminders after the phone restarts. Reminders are included in the backup file you choose to make.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

Scheme list updates

At most once a day, and when you tap "Check for scheme updates", the app downloads the latest scheme list (a public file of scheme names, rules and official links) from https://priya4042.github.io/data/yojana-data.json, our website on GitHub Pages. Nothing about you is sent: it is a normal web request, so GitHub, like any website, sees your IP address and device type. The file is checked before it is used and is stored on your phone; your answers never leave the phone.

Official websites and helplines

When you open a scheme's official website, it opens in your browser. That website's own privacy policy applies there. When you tap a helpline number, it opens in your phone's dialler; the app does not make or record the call.

When you share a scheme or your list, the text is handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
