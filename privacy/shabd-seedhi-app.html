Shabd Seedhi privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Shabd Seedhi is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your level stars, your daily ladder results, your streak and streak freezes, and your choices (app language, word language, sound, vibration, high contrast and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All word lists and ladders ship inside the app. The daily ladder is picked from the date, so everyone gets the same one and the game works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share result on a daily ladder, the app makes a picture of your result card and a short line (the day number, steps, par, stars and streak, never the words) and hands them to the app you choose through Android's share screen.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob only between ladders: now and then a full-screen ad when you move on from a finished ladder (never while you are climbing, at most once every three ladders, and not on your first open). There is no banner. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.
