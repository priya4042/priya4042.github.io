Daily Paheli privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Daily Paheli is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your daily results (solved or not, tries, hints, the language you read it in), your streak and streak freezes, the practice riddles you have solved, your badges and your choices (app language, riddle language, sound, vibration, high contrast, reduce motion and reminder time) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All riddles ship inside the app. The riddle of the day is picked from the date, so everyone gets the same one and the game works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Daily reminder (optional)

The reminder is off until you turn it on in Settings. Android then asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is set on your phone (and set again after the phone restarts); nothing is sent anywhere.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share result, the app makes a picture of your result card and a short line (the day number, stars, tries, hints and streak, never the riddle or the answer) and hands them to the app you choose through Android's share screen.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad after you finish a riddle (never while a riddle is open, at most once every three riddles, and not on your first open). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience, with a kids' shelf of easy riddles. It is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

आपके फ़ोन पर ही

आपके रोज़ के नतीजे, लगातार दिन और फ़्रीज़, हल की गई अभ्यास पहेलियाँ, बैज और आपकी पसंद (ऐप की भाषा, पहेली की भाषा, आवाज़, कंपन, याद का समय) सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन पर रहते हैं। कोई अकाउंट नहीं, कोई सर्वर नहीं। हम इन्हें कभी नहीं देखते और न कहीं भेजते हैं। सारी पहेलियाँ ऐप के अंदर हैं; आज की पहेली तारीख़ से चुनी जाती है, इसलिए बिना इंटरनेट भी चलता है।

रोज़ की याद (आपकी मर्ज़ी)

याद तब तक बंद रहती है जब तक आप सेटिंग में उसे चालू न करें। तब Android सूचना की अनुमति माँगता है। याद फ़ोन पर ही लगती है; कुछ भी बाहर नहीं जाता।

नतीजा शेयर करना

शेयर करने पर ऐप नतीजे के कार्ड की तस्वीर और एक छोटी पंक्ति (दिन का नंबर, तारे, कोशिशें, संकेत और लगातार दिन) बनाकर आपके चुने ऐप को देता है। पहेली या जवाब कभी नहीं।

विज्ञापन

ऐप में Google AdMob के विज्ञापन हैं: सिर्फ़ होम पेज पर बैनर, और कभी-कभी पहेली पूरी होने के बाद पूरी स्क्रीन वाला विज्ञापन (पहेली खुली हो तब कभी नहीं, तीन पहेलियों में ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा एक बार)। AdMob विज्ञापन दिखाने के लिए विज्ञापन ID जैसी जानकारी ले सकता है: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
