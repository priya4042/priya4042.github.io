Reflex Rush privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Reflex Rush is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your personal bests, Reflex score, Daily Rush scores and streak, the badges you have earned, a few counters (how many games you finished) and your choices (language, sound, vibration, reduce motion, colour-blind-safe colours, sound and touch cues, and the daily reminder and its time) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Every game runs on your phone, so the app works without internet. The Daily Rush picks the same games for everyone from the date alone; nothing is sent to do that.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Daily reminder

The Daily Rush reminder is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, your phone shows one notification a day at the time you pick; on Android 13 and newer the app asks for permission to show notifications first. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere, and you can turn it off at any time. The app does not use exact alarms.

Screen reader

TalkBack labels and spoken game updates are handled by your phone's own screen reader. The app sends nothing anywhere to do that.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, now and then a full-screen ad between sessions after a game, Daily Rush or duel is finished and closed (never during a game, not on your first open, at most once every two sessions), and an optional video you can choose to watch to continue a game once. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

Reflex Rush is made for teens and young adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Light sensitivity

Nothing in the app flashes more than three times a second and the screen never strobes. If you are sensitive to light, turn on Reduce motion and stop playing if you feel unwell.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps, sharing and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters. "Share my score" and "Share with friends" hand a short text to the app you choose through Android's share screen.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://priya4042.github.io/privacy/reflex-rush-app.html with a new date.
