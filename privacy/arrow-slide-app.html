Arrow Slide privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Arrow Slide is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your level progress, the stars you earned on each level, the levels you have opened, your daily puzzle results and streak, how many hints, undos and removes you have left today, and your choices (language and sound) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Every level and every daily puzzle is made inside the app from its number or date, so the game works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing.

Sharing your daily result

When you tap Share on the daily puzzle card, the app makes a picture of the card and a short line ("Daily puzzle #N cleared with M mistakes") and hands them to the app you choose through Android's share screen. What happens next is up to you and that app. The card never shows the puzzle board.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the menu pages, and now and then a full-screen ad between two levels (never while you are playing a level, and not in your first few sessions). If you run out of hints, you can choose to watch a rewarded video for more hints. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
