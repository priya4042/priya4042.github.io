Bijli Paani privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Bijli Paani is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your places, meters and cylinders (names, consumer numbers, board or company names), meter readings, bills, refills, tariffs you typed, photos of meters and bills, and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them. Android's own cloud backup of the app's data is switched off.

Photos

A photo of a meter or a bill is taken or picked only when you tap to add one, through Android's camera app or photo picker. Only the picture you choose is kept, made smaller, inside the app. No storage or camera permission is used.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and sets them on the phone for due dates, reading days and LPG refills. They are worked out from your own entries on the phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Tariffs and estimates

The app does not know or download official tariffs. Rates are typed by you, and any amount the app works out is an estimate, not a bill.

Files you share or save

The yearly PDF, Excel and CSV files and backup files are made on the phone. They leave it only when you share them (through Android's share screen, to the app you pick) or save them to a place you pick. A backup file contains all your readings, bills and photos, so keep it somewhere safe.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on the home and reports pages only. There are no ads on forms, readings or bills, and no full-screen ads. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. Your readings, bills and photos are never given to AdMob. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

Children

Bijli Paani is a household tool for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything it kept. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.
