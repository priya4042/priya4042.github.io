Bhakti Dhara privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Bhakti Dhara is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your favourite videos, your playlists (their names and the videos in them), the list of videos you watched with the place you stopped in each, your daily goal with how long you watched each day and the days the goal was met (for your streak), your japa mala counts, your morning and evening puja lists, your reminder choices and times, your family dates by tithi, reading plans, path counts, Ram naam lekhan notebook, tirth yatra ticks and notes, milestones, and your choices (language, theme, large text, city) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. For "My Bhakti Year" the app also keeps a small count for each day (minutes and videos played, verses read, mala beads, stars, finished plans and sankalps) for about 400 days; the summary is worked out on your phone. We never see, collect or upload them. Uninstalling the app removes them.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

How videos play

The app does not host, download or store any video. Every video stays on YouTube and is played through YouTube's official embedded player inside the app, with YouTube's own controls, branding and ads. Videos pause when you leave the app or turn the screen off.

While a video plays, the YouTube player is run by YouTube (Google), and YouTube's Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) apply to that playback. YouTube may use cookies and collect information such as your device, IP address and what you watch, as described in Google's policy. If you are signed in to YouTube on your phone, YouTube may link what you watch to your account according to your YouTube settings.

The small pictures next to each video in the lists are loaded from YouTube's picture server (i.ytimg.com), which receives an ordinary web request from your phone.

"Watch live on YouTube" and "Watch on YouTube" open the YouTube app or website, where YouTube's terms and policy apply.

The video list

The list of videos is inside the app, so lists open without internet. Once a day the app may download a newer copy of the list from our website (priya4042.github.io). Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request.

Scripture downloads

The full texts in the Granth library (for example the Vedas, Ramayana and Mahabharata) are large, so they are downloaded only when you tap Download, from our website (priya4042.github.io), and kept on your phone so they read without internet. You can delete a download at any time. Nothing about you is sent with the download.

Backup

"Save a backup" and "Send the backup" make a file of what you saved in the app (favourites, playlists, dates, reading places, reminder choices and settings). The file is made only when you ask, on your phone. You choose where it goes: a folder through Android's own save screen, or an app you pick (such as WhatsApp or Drive) to send it to yourself. It is not sent to us, and it holds no account, device ID or anything secret. "Restore from a backup" reads only the file you pick. Downloaded texts are not inside the file.

Voice search

Voice search uses your phone's own speech service; the app does not use the microphone itself and receives only the words.

Your city for the panchang

The panchang uses a city you pick from a list. The app does not use GPS or your location; the city is kept only on your phone.

Sharing

"Send on WhatsApp" and "Share" hand the video's name and its youtube.com link to the app you choose (WhatsApp or another app from Android's share screen). "Share a picture" makes a picture card on your phone (the video's name, the deity's letter, the date and our web address) and hands it, with the youtube.com link, to the app you choose. What happens next is up to you and that app.

"Share with family" makes a link to our website (priya4042.github.io) that contains the list's name and the YouTube ids of its videos, and hands it to the app you choose. Anyone who gets the link can see that list. The page on our website only shows the list; it has no trackers and saves nothing.

Request a bhajan

"Request a bhajan" opens your own email app with the bhajan name, singer and link you typed already filled in, addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. Then we receive it as an ordinary email, with your email address, and use it only to consider adding that video.

Reminders

If you turn on the morning or evening aarti reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone, one for each day of the week, naming that day's aarti. It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. Tapping a reminder opens that aarti. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings.

Vrat reminders (the evening before and the morning of the vrats you pick) and the evening nudge for your daily goal (only on days the goal is not met yet) work the same way: they are scheduled on your phone and stay off until you turn them on. Vrat dates are worked out in advance for New Delhi and are part of the app; nothing is looked up online.

Links

"Read the words", "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" and the website link open our website in your browser. Channel names open that channel on YouTube. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob on list screens only. No AdMob ad is shown on the screen where a video plays (the YouTube player may show YouTube's own ads, which YouTube controls). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and families, and is not directed at children under 13. The Bal Bhakti screen lists children's bhajans and stories for parents to play with their children; it shows no ads and collects nothing.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

Not affiliated

Bhakti Dhara is not made by, or affiliated with, YouTube, Google, any temple trust or any music label. Videos belong to the channels that uploaded them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
