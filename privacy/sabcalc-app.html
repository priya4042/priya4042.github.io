Sab Calculator privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Sab Calculator is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type (numbers, dates, amounts), your favourite calculators, your recent calculations and the calculator history are stored only on your phone. All answers are worked out on the phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload any of it.

When you share an answer as text or as a picture, it is handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen (for example WhatsApp). What happens next is up to you and that app.

Rates and links

The jewellery and currency calculators use only the rates you type; the app does not fetch live rates. The "Official income tax calculator" button opens the Income Tax Department's website in your browser; the app sends it nothing.

Backups

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. Keep them somewhere safe. If you choose "Save backup to Google Drive or a folder", Android's own save screen opens and the file goes only where you pick (for example your Google Drive, under Google's policy). The app does not sign in to Drive or read anything else there. Restoring works the same way: you pick the file.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, the app asks your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) to confirm it is you. Android checks this and tells the app only whether it passed. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the "use biometric hardware" permission.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Sab Calculator widget to your home screen, it shows the last result of the calculator and the sum that made it. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. The numbers are kept on your phone only. Remove the widget to stop this.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
