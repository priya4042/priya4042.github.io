Beat Tap privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Beat Tap is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your best scores, grades and stars for each track and difficulty, your Daily Track score and streak, a few counters (how many songs you finished), your sync offset and your choices (language, sound, vibration, No-fail, reduce motion and colour-blind-safe lanes) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

The music

All the music is original and is made by the app on your phone; no songs are downloaded. The first time you play a track it is mixed into a sound file in the app's own cache folder, so it starts at once the next time. Only the last few tracks are kept, and Android may clear the cache at any time; the app simply mixes the track again. The app works without internet.

The Daily Track is chosen from the date alone, so everyone gets the same song; nothing is sent to do that.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's saved scores into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sync, sound and vibration

The sync screen plays clicks and times your taps on the screen. It does not use the microphone: the app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, now and then a full-screen ad after a finished song's result page has been closed (never during a song, not on your first open, at most once every two songs), and an optional video you can choose to watch to continue a song once after your health runs out. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

Beat Tap is made for teens and young adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Light sensitivity

The beat pulse behind the lanes is soft and never comes more than three times a second, and the screen never strobes. If you are sensitive to light, turn on Reduce motion and stop playing if you feel unwell.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps, sharing and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters. "Share my Daily Track" and "Share with friends" hand a short text to the app you choose through Android's share screen.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://priya4042.github.io/privacy/beat-tap-app.html with a new date.
