Beat Tap privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Beat Tap is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your best scores, grades and stars for each track and difficulty, your Daily Track score and streak, a few counters (how many songs you finished), your badges, your sync offset and your choices (language, sound, vibration, No-fail, Accessible mode, reduce motion, colour-blind-safe lanes and the reminder time) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

The music

All the music is original and is made by the app on your phone; no songs or sound files are downloaded. The first time you play a track it is mixed into a sound file in the app's own cache folder, so it starts at once the next time. Only the last few tracks are kept, and Android may clear the cache at any time; the app simply mixes the track again. The app works without internet.

The Daily Track is chosen from the date alone, so everyone gets the same song; nothing is sent to do that.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's saved scores into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Track packs from our website

Once a day at most, when the phone is online, the app reads a small list file from priya4042.github.io/content/beat-tap/ and downloads any new track pack listed there; you can also check by hand in Settings, Get new tracks. A track pack is a small text file of musical recipes (tempo, scale, instruments and patterns) that the app checks strictly and then turns into music on your phone, exactly like the built-in tracks. It holds no code and no sound files. Nothing about you is sent; the request is an ordinary file download, so like any web request it shows your IP address to the website host (GitHub). Downloaded packs are kept on the phone and play offline. The app works fully without them.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the Daily Track reminder in Settings (it is off until you do), the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and schedules one local notification a day at the time you pick. It is set on your phone only; no server is involved and nothing is sent. Turn it off in Settings at any time.

Sync, sound and vibration

The sync screen plays clicks and times your taps on the screen. It does not use the microphone: the app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing. Vibration, including the buzz on every note in Accessible mode, is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, now and then a full-screen ad after a finished song's result page has been closed (never during a song, not on your first open, at most once every two songs), and an optional video you can choose to watch to continue a song once after your health runs out. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

Beat Tap is made for teens and young adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Light sensitivity

The beat pulse behind the lanes is soft and never comes more than three times a second, and the screen never strobes. If you are sensitive to light, turn on Reduce motion and stop playing if you feel unwell.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps, sharing and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters. "Share my Daily Track" and "Share with friends" hand a short text to the app you choose through Android's share screen.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version and track pack downloads above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://priya4042.github.io/privacy/beat-tap-app.html with a new date.

संगीत

सारा संगीत ओरिजिनल है और ऐप इसे आपके फ़ोन पर ही बनाता है; कोई गाना या साउंड फ़ाइल डाउनलोड नहीं होती। किसी ट्रैक को पहली बार खेलने पर वह ऐप के अपने कैश फ़ोल्डर में एक साउंड फ़ाइल के रूप में मिक्स होता है, ताकि अगली बार तुरंत शुरू हो। सिर्फ़ आख़िरी कुछ ट्रैक रखे जाते हैं, और Android कभी भी कैश साफ़ कर सकता है; तब ऐप ट्रैक फिर से मिक्स कर लेता है। ऐप इंटरनेट के बिना चलता है।

डेली ट्रैक सिर्फ़ तारीख़ से चुना जाता है, इसलिए सबको एक ही गाना मिलता है; इसके लिए कुछ नहीं भेजा जाता।

अगर आपके फ़ोन में Google बैकअप चालू है, तो Android ऐप के सहेजे स्कोर आपके अपने Google अकाउंट के बैकअप में रख सकता है। वह बैकअप आपका है; हम उसे कभी नहीं देखते।

हमारी वेबसाइट से ट्रैक पैक

फ़ोन ऑनलाइन होने पर दिन में ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा एक बार ऐप priya4042.github.io/content/beat-tap/ से एक छोटी सूची फ़ाइल पढ़ता है और उसमें दिए नए ट्रैक पैक डाउनलोड करता है; आप सेटिंग में "नए ट्रैक लाएँ" से ख़ुद भी देख सकते हैं। ट्रैक पैक संगीत की रेसिपी (रफ़्तार, सुर, साज़ और पैटर्न) वाली एक छोटी टेक्स्ट फ़ाइल है, जिसे ऐप अच्छी तरह जाँचकर आपके फ़ोन पर ही संगीत में बदलता है, बिल्कुल ऐप के अपने ट्रैक की तरह। इसमें कोई कोड या साउंड फ़ाइल नहीं होती। आपके बारे में कुछ नहीं भेजा जाता; यह एक सामान्य फ़ाइल डाउनलोड है, इसलिए किसी भी वेब अनुरोध की तरह वेबसाइट होस्ट (GitHub) को आपका IP पता दिखता है। डाउनलोड हुए पैक फ़ोन पर रहते हैं और बिना इंटरनेट चलते हैं। ऐप इनके बिना भी पूरा चलता है।

रोज़ का रिमाइंडर

अगर आप सेटिंग में डेली ट्रैक रिमाइंडर चालू करते हैं (जब तक आप चालू न करें, यह बंद रहता है), तो ऐप Android से सूचनाएँ दिखाने की अनुमति माँगता है और आपके चुने समय पर रोज़ एक लोकल सूचना तय करता है। यह सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन पर होता है; कोई सर्वर नहीं, कुछ नहीं भेजा जाता। सेटिंग में इसे कभी भी बंद करें।
