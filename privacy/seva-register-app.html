Seva Register privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Seva Register is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your organisation's profile (name, address, registration numbers, PAN, logo and signature), receipts, donors, members and their fees, volunteers, events, check-ins, funds, expenses and bill photos, and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them. Android's own cloud backup is switched off for this app, so your records leave the phone only when you send them yourself.

Donors', members' and volunteers' details

Names, phone numbers, addresses and PANs that you write are kept on your phone only and used only when you choose: to print them on a receipt, statement or certificate, to open WhatsApp with a message you can read before sending, or to open the phone dialler. The app does not read your contacts and has no SMS permission. Please collect only what your organisation needs and keep it safe.

Camera

The camera is used only when you tap to scan volunteer cards at the check-in kiosk or to photograph a bill. Android asks for permission at that moment, after the app explains why. Scanned codes and bill photos stay on the phone. You can say no and still use the app.

Photos

A logo, a signature or a bill photo from the gallery is chosen with Android's photo picker. Only the picture you choose is read, made small and kept inside the app.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app asks to show notifications and sets reminders on the phone for fee due days and events. Nothing is sent to members by itself.

Files you share or save

Receipts, statements, certificates, volunteer cards, reports, Excel and CSV files and backup files are made on the phone. They leave it only when you share them (through Android's share screen, to the app you pick) or save them where you choose. A backup file contains all your records, including the secret used to sign volunteer cards, so keep it safe.

Advertising and tracking

None. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

Tax

The app makes receipts and keeps records. It does not file Form 10BD or issue Form 10BE and does not decide any tax benefit. Organisations must follow the Income Tax rules that apply to them.

Children

Seva Register is a tool for adults running organisations. It is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything it kept. Backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

दानदाताओं, सदस्यों और स्वयंसेवकों की जानकारी

आपके लिखे नाम, फ़ोन नंबर, पते और PAN सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन में रहते हैं और तभी इस्तेमाल होते हैं जब आप चाहें: रसीद, विवरण या प्रमाण पत्र पर छापने, WhatsApp पर संदेश खोलने (भेजने से पहले आप पढ़ सकते हैं), या डायलर खोलने के लिए। ऐप आपके कॉन्टैक्ट नहीं पढ़ता और उसे SMS की अनुमति नहीं है। कृपया उतनी ही जानकारी लें जितनी संस्था को चाहिए, और उसे सुरक्षित रखें।
