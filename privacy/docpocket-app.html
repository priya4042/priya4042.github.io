Document Pocket privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Document Pocket is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Document Pocket is not a government app. It is not linked to, or endorsed by, any government department or service. The pictures and PDFs you keep in it are your own personal copies; they are not verified or legally valid documents.

What stays on your phone

The family members' names and relations, the documents you add (photos and PDF files of their pages, the document type, name, document number, expiry date and notes), your favourites and your office kits are stored only on your phone, in the app's private folder. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

The pages and the list of documents are encrypted on your phone with a key that is created on your phone and kept in Android's secure key storage. Document numbers are shown hidden (for example XXXX XXXX 1234) unless you tap to show them.

Camera, photos and files

When you add a page, the app opens your phone's own camera app, photo picker or file picker. Only the pictures or PDF files you take or choose are read, encrypted and saved in the app's private folder; the camera's own temporary copy is deleted. The app has no camera or storage permission.

Cropping, turning, the scan clean-up and making photos smaller are all done on your phone; nothing is uploaded. The original photo is only replaced when you tap "Use photo", and no extra copies are left behind. When a photo is saved, the camera details stored inside it (such as the place it was taken and the phone model) are removed. Photos are made smaller, to at most 2000 pixels on the long side, unless you turn on "Keep full quality" in Settings.

Sharing and printing

When you share documents, the app makes a temporary copy of the pages you chose (as pictures or as one PDF) and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share screen. That temporary copy is deleted from your phone when you come back to Document Pocket, and again each time the app starts. What happens after you share is up to you and the app you shared with. When you print, the pages go to Android's own print screen. If you add a purpose line (for example "For bank account opening only"), it is printed on the shared PDF pages.

Screenshots

Screens that show your documents block screenshots and screen recording, and hide the app's preview in the recent apps list.

Backups

Backups are files you make yourself, locked with a password you choose, and saved where you pick in Android's save screen (for example Google Drive or a folder) or sent through the share screen. The app has no Google sign-in and uploads nothing itself. The backup contains all members, documents, numbers and pages, encrypted with your password. If you forget the password, the backup cannot be opened; nobody, including us, can recover it.

Google auto-backup is turned off

Because the documents are sensitive, Document Pocket turns off Android's automatic backup to your Google account and the copying of its data when you move to a new phone. The documents and the key that opens them never leave the phone this way. To move to a new phone, make a backup file and restore it there.

App lock

App lock is offered switched on when you first open the app, and you can turn it off. When it is on, the app asks Android to check your phone's own fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. Android does the check and only tells the app whether it passed; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the biometric permission.

Reminders

If you turn on expiry reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone for 30 days and 7 days before each expiry date you entered, and on the day. It re-schedules them after the phone restarts. The notification shows the document type and the person's name, never the document number. You can turn reminders off at any time.

Home-screen widget

Document Pocket has no home-screen widget, so no document details are ever shown on your home screen.

More apps

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page opens our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. Ads never receive your documents or anything you type in the app. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
