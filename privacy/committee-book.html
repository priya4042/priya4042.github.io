Committee Book privacy policy

Last updated: 2026

Committee Book ("the app") is developed by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

The app works offline. The committees you create, members, payment records and settings are stored locally on your phone. We do not collect or send this to us.

Optional live sync (Supabase)

If the organiser turns on live sharing, the committee's data (committee name, member names, phone numbers you enter, amounts, and who paid) is stored in the organiser's own Supabase database so that approved members can see their status. Access is controlled by security rules: only the organiser and members the organiser approves can read a committee. If you never share a committee, no data leaves your device.

Members' phone numbers

Organisers may enter members' phone numbers to send reminders. These are used only to open WhatsApp / SMS / your dialer on your device, and (if live sync is on) to identify members within that committee.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app itself does not collect this.

Notifications

The app schedules local reminders (collection due, your turn) on your device.

No accounts / no personal login

The app does not require an email or password. Live sync uses anonymous sign-in (a random device identifier), not your personal identity.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Use the app fully offline (don't enable live sharing).
  • Delete a committee to remove its records.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
