Yaad Jodi privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Yaad Jodi is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your games played, pairs found, fewest moves for each grid, daily game results and streak, your badges, your last choices of deck and grid, and your settings (app language, look, huge cards, names under pictures, slow card turns, the look at all cards before a game, sound, vibration and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All cards are drawn inside the app and every game is dealt on the phone, so it works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permission. It uses the internet only for ads and the new version notice below.

Sharing your daily score

When you tap Share my score, the phone's own share sheet opens with a short line such as your daily game number and moves. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with, and the line never shows where the cards were.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad after you close the result of a finished game (never while a game is open, never over the result, not on the first open, at most once every three finished games and six minutes). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience, especially older players, and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
