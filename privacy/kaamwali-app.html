Kaamwali Hisaab privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Kaamwali Hisaab is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The staff you add (names, kind of work, phone numbers, work timings, joining and last working dates, salary terms, weekly offs, paid leaves, salary day, the language for their summary, and, if you write them, a note about the ID proof they gave and an emergency contact number), their daily attendance marks with the time each mark was last changed, the holidays you mark for everyone, and the money entries you write (salary paid, advances, loans and their monthly cuts, loans paid back and bonuses, with dates, payment mode and notes) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

When you share a salary slip as a picture or PDF, a month summary as text, or a year's account as an Excel (CSV) file, it is handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen. When you send a summary on WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp with the staff member's number and the text already filled in; you press send yourself. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself, either through the share screen or through Android's own save screen, where you can pick Google Drive or any folder. The app has no Google sign-in and does not upload anything itself. Backups contain all your staff, their attendance, payments, holidays, ID proof notes and emergency contact numbers, so keep them somewhere safe. When you restore a file with "Add to mine", it is joined with the data on your phone, on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

The ID proof note is only text you type, such as "Aadhaar copy given". The app never asks for, scans or stores a photo of any document.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

Calling a staff member

The call button opens your phone's dialer with their number, and the WhatsApp button opens a WhatsApp chat with that number. You place the call or send the message yourself; the app does not call, send or read anything.

Phone contacts

When you tap the contacts button while adding a staff member, Android's own contact picker opens. Only the one contact you choose (its name and number) is handed to the app and saved on your phone. The app has no permission to read your contact list and never uploads contacts.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, the app asks Android to check your phone's own fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. Android does the check and only tells the app whether it passed; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the biometric permission.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Kaamwali Hisaab widget to your home screen, it shows today's date, how many of your staff are marked and the names of those not yet marked. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. Remove the widget if you do not want that.

Reminders

If you turn on salary day reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone for each staff member's salary day. Each reminder shows the staff member's name and the amount due, worked out on your phone; anyone who can see your notifications can see it. It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time from the menu or Settings.

More apps

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page opens our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
