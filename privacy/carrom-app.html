Carrom Corner privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Carrom Corner is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your wins against the computer, pass-and-play games, trick shots solved, a game left part way, and your choices (language, sound, vibration, reduce motion, Big & calm, Pro mode and points match) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

The game, the computer player and the trick shots all run inside the app, so the app works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The wooden sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad after a finished match (never during a match or practice, not on the first open, at most once every two finished matches and five minutes). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
