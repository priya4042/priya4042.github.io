Antakshari Night privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Antakshari Night is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Team names and colours, your game choices (letters, time, rounds, special rounds), your records (games played, songs sung, best streak), the badges you earn and your settings (app language, sound, vibration and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

The app contains no songs, lyrics or recordings. You sing from memory; the app only keeps the turns, letters, time and scores. It works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds, vibration and screen

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing; your singing is never heard or stored by the app. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback. During a game the screen is kept awake, and the TV board turns the screen sideways.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad after a finished game (never during a game, not in your first session, at most once every two finished games, three a day and ten minutes apart). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is a family party game meant for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13; younger children should play with an adult holding the phone.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Screen readers

When a screen reader such as TalkBack is on, the app tells it about turns, letters, time left and scores. This uses the phone's own accessibility service; nothing is recorded or sent.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
