Rashan List privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Rashan List is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter (lists, items, amounts, notes, prices, shop names, usual lists, the running-out list, your budget, at-home stock counts and bill photos) is stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload it.

When you share a list as text or a picture, it is handed to WhatsApp or the app you choose through Android's share screen. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Pasting a list

The app reads your clipboard only when you tap "Paste from clipboard" on the Paste a list screen. The text is read on your phone to make the items and is not sent anywhere.

Bill photos

If you add a bill photo, the app opens your phone's own camera app or Android's photo picker. Only the one picture you take or pick is given to Rashan List, which makes it smaller and keeps a copy in its own private folder on your phone. The app has no camera or storage permission and cannot see your other photos. Deleting the photo, or the list it belongs to, removes the copy.

Voice entry

If you tap the mic, the app opens your phone's own speech recogniser (for example Google's). That recogniser records your voice and turns it into text under its own privacy policy, and may use the internet to do so. Rashan List receives only the text, shows you the items it understood and adds nothing until you confirm. Rashan List does not record or keep any audio and has no microphone permission.

Backups

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. Keep them somewhere safe. If you choose "Save backup to Google Drive or a folder", Android's own save screen opens and the file goes only where you pick (for example your Google Drive, under Google's policy). The app does not sign in to Drive or read anything else there. Restoring works the same way: you pick the file. Bill photos are put inside the backup file when they add up to 15 MB or less; if they are bigger, the app tells you and leaves them out.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, Rashan List asks your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) to confirm it is you. Android checks this and tells the app only whether it passed. Rashan List never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the "use biometric hardware" permission.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Rashan List widget to your home screen, it shows how many items are left to buy and the first few names. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. The text is kept on your phone only. Remove the widget to stop this.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
