MCA Entrance Prep privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

MCA Entrance Prep is a study app for MCA entrance exams (NIMCET, CUET PG, MAH MCA CET, WB JECA and Jamia MCA), made by Priya Tech Labs.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no ads. We do not run any server that receives your data.

What stays on your phone

Your study progress, quiz history, notes, mistake journal and mistake tags, college choice list, exam-day checklist and centre address, settings and your optional Claude API key are stored only on your device. PDFs you export (formula sheets, choice lists, weekly reports) are made on your phone and shared only where you choose.

When the app uses the internet

  • News: it downloads public headlines from Google News (news.google.com). Only a search query such as "NIMCET" is sent.
  • Exam dates: it downloads a public list of expected exam dates from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Nothing about you is sent.
  • Links: when you tap a link (official sites, past papers, free resources, online tests, YouTube lessons, Google Maps, Google Search, Google Calendar, Google Translate) it opens in your browser or that app, under that site's own privacy policy.
  • AI tutor (optional): only if you add your own Claude API key and press "Solve", the question text and any photo you attach are sent to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) to generate the answer. This uses your key and is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy. Nothing is sent if you never use this feature.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you about study time, exam dates and, if you turn it on in the Exam Day Kit, the evening before and morning of your exam. You can turn these off in Settings.
  • Camera / photos: only when you choose to photograph a question for the AI tutor.
  • Internet: for the features listed above.

Backups

If Android backup is on in your phone settings, Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account. You can also export a backup file yourself; where you save it is your choice.

Children

The app is meant for college students and graduates preparing for MCA entrance exams. It collects no personal data from anyone.
