DotNet Interview Prep privacy policy

Last updated: 2026

DotNet Interview Prep ("the app") is developed by Priya Tech Labs. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

The app itself does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information on our servers. All your study data (progress, bookmarks, saved questions, and STAR stories) is stored only on your own device.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect and use certain data, such as your device's advertising ID, to show relevant ads. This is handled by Google, not by us. You can learn more and control ad personalization in your device settings and in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

The app may request permission to show notifications (for optional study reminders). You can turn these off any time in the app settings or your device settings.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. We do not have access to it.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: priyatechlabs9@gmail.com
