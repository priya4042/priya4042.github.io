Cricket Captain privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Cricket Captain is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your career numbers (matches, runs, fours, sixes, highest score and so on), your Daily Challenge results and streak, the tournament you are playing, the badges you have earned, a few counters (how many matches you finished) and your choices (language, difficulty, sound, vibration, reduce motion, beat cues) are saved only on your phone. We have no server and no accounts, and we cannot see any of it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Every match runs on your phone, so the app works without internet. The Daily Challenge makes the same balls and target for everyone from the date alone; nothing is sent to do that.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds (bat on ball, the crowd, the stumps, the beat cue ticks) are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Screen reader

TalkBack labels and spoken ball-by-ball results are handled by your phone's own screen reader. The app sends nothing anywhere to do that.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only, and now and then a full-screen ad after a finished match screen is closed (never during a match or on a result, not on the first open, at most every 2 matches and 4 minutes). If you choose to, you can watch a short video once in a match to bat on with one extra wicket; it is never shown unless you tap for it, and never in the Daily Challenge. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads; see Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

Cricket Captain is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Made-up teams

All teams, players, kits and grounds in the game are made up. The app has no link with any real league, board, team or player.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps, sharing and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters. "Share my result" and "Share with friends" open your phone's share sheet; you choose where the text goes, and it holds only your result squares and a link, never the target or anything about you.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://priya4042.github.io/privacy/cricket-captain-app.html with a new date.
