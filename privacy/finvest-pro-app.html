Finvest Pro privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Finvest Pro is an educational tool. It is not investment advice, not a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and its developer is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst. Estimates depend on your assumptions and may be wrong. Consult a SEBI-registered adviser before investing.

Finvest Pro helps you learn how stock valuation models work. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps these items in the phone's local app storage only:

  • Your Beginner or Detailed view choice.
  • Whether you have seen and accepted the first-run notice.
  • Companies you save to your list.
  • Holdings you type into the portfolio notebook (company, quantity, cost per share).
  • When live figures are turned on in a build, a 12-hour cache of the figures it fetched.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it.

What leaves your device

The standard version uses built-in sample figures and sends nothing over the network.

Some builds can include optional live figures. These come from Alpha Vantage (https://www.alphavantage.co), a third-party data provider. In those builds, when you ask for live figures, the app sends the ticker symbol you typed and the app's data key to Alpha Vantage. Alpha Vantage also receives your IP address, as with any web request. No name, email, contact, location or device identifier is sent. Alpha Vantage's own privacy policy applies to that request.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.
  • No payments.

Children

The app is meant for adults and students learning finance. It does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
