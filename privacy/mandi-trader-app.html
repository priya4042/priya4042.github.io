Mandi Trader privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Mandi Trader is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your stars and best result for each season, the trips in progress, your savings and cart upgrades, your daily market results and streak, and your choices (language, sound, vibration, high contrast and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All coins in the game are play money. They cannot be bought, sold or turned into real money, and the app has no in-app purchases. It is a game, not real trading: the prices are made up by the game and are not real mandi prices.

The prices, news and festivals are made inside the app from fixed numbers or the date, so the game works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The coin, bell and newspaper sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share on the daily market card, the app makes a picture of the card with a short line ("Daily market #N: 1,240 play coins after 10 days") and hands them to the app you choose through Android's share screen. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: now and then a full-screen ad between days (never while you trade, at most once every six days, and not in your first session). There are no banner ads. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent: no account, no ID and no data from the app, just an ordinary web request to the site. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play, which updates it for you.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
