Shagun Register privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Shagun Register is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The people, places, phone numbers, family sizes, functions, guest lists (with replies and how many adults and children are coming), invitation messages, gifts (cash and gifts in kind with their rough value), gift photos, your return-gift rule, function budgets (expense heads, vendor names and phone numbers, amounts and due dates), to-do lists (tasks, who is doing them and due dates) and vendor contacts (names, phone numbers, kind of work and notes) you enter are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

When you share a list, a budget, a function's register as a PDF or its total as a picture, or export it to Excel, the file or text is handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen. When you print a register, it goes to Android's own print screen. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself, either through the share screen or through Android's own save screen, where you can pick Google Drive or any folder. The app has no Google sign-in and does not upload anything itself. Backups contain everyone's names, gifts, guest lists, your function budgets, to-do lists and vendor contacts, so keep them somewhere safe. When you add a family member's backup to your register, it is read on your phone and merged there. Gift photos are not included in backup files; they stay on the phone where they were taken and are deleted when you delete the gift or uninstall the app.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also copy the app's data into your own Google account backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it. You can turn it off in Settings, Google, Backup.

Invitations on WhatsApp

When you send an invitation, the app opens WhatsApp (or the share screen, when the family has no number) with the guest's number and your message already filled in. You press send yourself in WhatsApp. The app does not send anything on its own and does not read your WhatsApp.

Calling a vendor

The call button opens your phone's dialer with the vendor's number, and the WhatsApp button opens a WhatsApp chat with that number. You place the call or send the message yourself; the app does not call, send or read anything.

Phone contacts

When you tap "Add from phone contacts" (or the contacts button for a person or a vendor), Android's own contact picker opens. Only the one contact you choose (its name and number) is handed to the app and saved in your register on your phone. The app has no permission to read your contact list and never uploads contacts.

Gift photos

When you add a photo to a gift, the app opens your phone's own camera app or photo picker. Only the one picture you take or choose is copied into the app's private folder on your phone. The app has no camera or storage permission and never uploads photos.

Speaking to add an entry

When you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech recognition screen opens (on most phones this is provided by Google). It listens and hands the app only the text it heard; the app itself does not record audio and has no microphone permission. How the speech service handles your voice is covered by its provider's privacy policy, for Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

App lock

If you turn on app lock, the app asks Android to check your phone's own fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. Android does the check and only tells the app whether it passed; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN. This uses the biometric permission.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Shagun Register widget to your home screen, it shows the name and date of your coming or latest function and the shagun received there. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. Remove the widget if you do not want that.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone for the day before each function, each vendor payment due date and each to-do due date. It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time from the menu.

More apps

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page opens our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
