Shagun Register privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Shagun Register is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The people, places, phone numbers, family sizes, functions, guest lists and gifts you enter are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

When you share a list or export it to Excel, the file or text is handed to the app you choose through Android's share screen. What happens next is up to you and that app.

Backups are files that you create and save yourself. They contain everyone's names and gifts, so keep them somewhere safe.

Phone contacts

When you tap "Add from phone contacts", Android's own contact picker opens. Only the one contact you choose (its name and number) is handed to the app and saved in your register on your phone. The app has no permission to read your contact list and never uploads contacts.

Reminders

If you turn on function reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone for the day before each function. It also re-schedules them after the phone restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time from the menu.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
