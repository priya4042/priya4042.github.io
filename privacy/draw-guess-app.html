Draw & Guess privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Draw & Guess (shown on Google Play as "Draw and Guess") is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The player names and faces you type in for a party game, your last game settings (players, teams, rounds, timer, difficulty and word packs), the list of recently drawn words (so the next games avoid repeats), the practice words you drew today, the drawings you save to your gallery, and your choices (language, word language, sound, vibration and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

All words ship inside the app and the daily practice list is picked inside the app from the date, so the game works without internet.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Your drawings

Drawings are kept as lines and colours inside the app's own storage on your phone. Deleting a drawing in the gallery removes it from the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of them.

When you tap "Share as picture", the app turns that one drawing into a PNG picture with a short line ("My drawing of ...") and hands it to the app you choose through Android's share screen. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share and pick an app.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing. Guesses are said aloud between the players; the app does not listen.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page, and now and then a full-screen ad between two party games (at most every second game, never during a game, never during practice, and not in your first session). AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

The words are family friendly, but the app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
