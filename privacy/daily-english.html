Daily English privacy policy

Last updated: 2026

Daily English ("the app") is developed by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

The app does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information on our servers. All your learning data (progress, favorites, streak, settings) is stored only on your own device.

Microphone

The app uses your microphone ONLY for the pronunciation practice feature, to check how you say a word or sentence. This happens on your device using the system's speech recognition. We do not record, upload, or store your audio.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads. This is handled by Google, not by us. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data leaves your phone for this.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

The app is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here.
