Shaadi Planner privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Shaadi Planner is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your plan (names, dates and budget), functions and venues, guest families with their phone numbers, answers, food and stay needs, budget lines and payments, vendors, checklist tasks, tables and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them. Android's own cloud backup of the app's data is switched off, so your plan does not leave the phone unless you send it yourself.

Guests' and vendors' details

Names and phone numbers you type or import are kept on your phone only and used only when you choose: to open WhatsApp with an invitation you can read before sending, to open the phone dialler, or to put them in a file you share. The app does not read your contacts and has no SMS or call log permission.

Files you open, share or save

A guest list you import is read once from the CSV or Excel file you pick. PDFs, Excel and CSV files and backup files are made on the phone. They leave it only when you share them (through Android's share screen, to the app you pick) or save them to a place you pick. A backup file contains your whole plan, so keep it somewhere safe.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and sets them on the phone for payments due and tasks due. Nothing is sent anywhere. Turn them off any time in Settings.

Maps and links

Opening a venue's map hands the venue name or your saved link to your maps app. Links to our website open in your browser.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on the home and budget pages only. There are no ads on forms, guest pages, seating or the checklist, and no full-screen ads. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. Your guests, money and plans are never given to AdMob. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

Children

Shaadi Planner is a planning tool for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything it kept. Backup and other files you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

आपके लिखे या इम्पोर्ट किए नाम और फ़ोन नंबर सिर्फ़ फ़ोन में रहते हैं और तभी इस्तेमाल होते हैं जब आप चाहें: WhatsApp पर निमंत्रण खोलने के लिए (भेजने से पहले आप पढ़ सकते हैं), डायलर खोलने के लिए, या आपकी शेयर की गई फ़ाइल में। ऐप आपके कॉन्टैक्ट नहीं पढ़ता और उसके पास SMS या कॉल लॉग की अनुमति नहीं है।
