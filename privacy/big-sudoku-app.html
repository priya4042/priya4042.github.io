Big Sudoku privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Big Sudoku is made by Priya Tech Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your puzzles in progress (numbers, notes and undo history), your stats (puzzles solved, best times, hints used, days in a row and the daily puzzle streak) and your choices (language, look, huge digits, digit style, left-handed layout, mistake checking, highlights, clock, sound, vibration and reduce motion) are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. We never see, collect or upload them.

Every puzzle is made on your phone, so the app works without internet. The daily puzzle is picked from the date alone, so everyone gets the same one; nothing is sent to do that.

If Backup by Google is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data into your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we never see it.

Sounds and vibration

The sounds are made by the app on your phone. The app has no microphone or camera permission and records nothing. Vibration is the phone's normal touch feedback and needs no permission.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home page only (never on the puzzle screen); now and then a full-screen ad after a solved puzzle's result has closed (never while you are solving, not on your first open, at most once every three solved puzzles); and an optional video you can choose to watch for one more hint. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads.

Children

Big Sudoku is made for adults, especially older players. It is not directed at children under 13.

New version notice

If you installed the app from our website, once a day it downloads a small version file from priya4042.github.io to tell you when a new version is ready. Nothing about you is sent. This is not done when the app was installed from Google Play.

More apps, sharing and links

The "More apps from Priya Tech Labs" page, the website and the privacy policy links open our website in your browser. The links carry no tracking parameters. "Share my result" and "Share with friends" hand a short text to the app you choose through Android's share screen; the daily result text never contains the puzzle's answer.

Nothing else is collected

Apart from what AdMob collects for ads and the version check above, the app collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://priya4042.github.io/privacy/big-sudoku-app.html with a new date.
