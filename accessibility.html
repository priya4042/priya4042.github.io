Accessibility

Last updated 20 October 2026

What this website does

  • Keyboard. Every link, button, form and the search can be used with the keyboard, with a clearly visible focus outline. A "skip to content" link is the first thing the Tab key reaches.
  • Screen readers. Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labels; images have text alternatives, and decorative ones are hidden from screen readers.
  • Less motion. If your device is set to reduce motion, the animations on this site switch off.
  • Colour and contrast. Text is chosen to be readable against its background, and there is a dark mode.
  • Language. The site - including all guides and app pages - can be switched to Hindi with the button at the top.
  • Zoom. Pages work when zoomed in and on small screens without sideways scrolling.

Known gaps

The case studies, privacy policies and press kit are in English only. Screenshots of the apps show the English versions.

Tell me about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use with your setup, email priyatechlabs9@gmail.com and say which page and what went wrong. I'll fix it.
