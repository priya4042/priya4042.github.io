How I work with you

From the first message to the app on Google Play.

  1. 1

    Tell me the idea

    A few lines on what you need and who will use it. I reply with questions, a plan and a quote.

  2. 2

    Plan the screens

    We agree what each screen does before any code is written, so there are no surprises.

  3. 3

    Build and test

    You try real builds on your phone as they come together, and your feedback shapes the next one.

  4. 4

    Launch and look after it

    Store listing, screenshots and privacy policy done, then updates when you need them.
