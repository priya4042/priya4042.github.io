How it's calculated

Mathematics: 50 questions x 12 marks, Reasoning: 40 x 6, Computer: 20 x 6, English: 10 x 4 - 1,000 marks in all. Each wrong answer takes away 25% of that question's marks (3 in Maths, 1.5 in Reasoning and Computer, 1 in English).
