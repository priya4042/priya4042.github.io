What it does

QuickScan reads every common QR code and barcode and knows what to do with it: join the Wi-Fi, pay with UPI, save the contact, add the event. It also makes codes - with your logo and a caption - and keeps everything you scan or create in one place.

Features

  • Smart results for Wi-Fi, UPI payments, contacts, calendar events and links
  • Create 12 code types, with your own logo and a caption under the code
  • My Codes wallet for the codes you use every day
  • History with favourites and search
  • Batch scanning with CSV export
  • Reads results aloud, in English and Hindi

Questions people ask

Is QuickScan free?

Yes. It is free with small ads.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Scanning and creating codes happen on your phone.

How do I get it before it's public?

Join the test: send your Gmail address and you'll get a Google Play invite link. Google asks new apps to run a 14-day test with at least 12 people.
