Spendly

Coming to Google Play

A private, offline money manager for income, spending, budgets and savings.

Androidv1.0.0English, HindiWorks offlineFree with ads

Screens

What it does

Spendly keeps your money on your phone, not on a server. Add what you earn and spend in a couple of taps, set budgets, track savings goals, and keep track of who owes whom.

Features

  • Wallets for cash, bank and cards
  • Budgets and recurring entries
  • Savings goals and a round-up jar
  • Udhaar: lend and borrow tracking
  • Bill splitting and EMI tracker
  • Charts, calendar heatmap, PIN or fingerprint lock

Questions people ask

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no server.

Can I lock the app?

Yes, with a PIN or your fingerprint.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes.
