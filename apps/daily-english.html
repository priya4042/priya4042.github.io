Daily English

Coming to Google Play

Learn to speak English with a coach that listens and checks your pronunciation.

Androidv1.0.0English, HindiWorks offlineFree with ads

What it does

Daily English is practice for speaking, not just reading. Say a word or a sentence and it tells you how close you were, with US and UK audio to copy.

Features

  • Speak and check: pronunciation scored by speech recognition
  • US and UK pronunciation audio
  • Grammar, word usage and everyday phrases
  • Spoken two-person dialogues
  • Quizzes and favourites
  • Daily reminder

Questions people ask

Is my voice recorded?

No. Speech is checked on your phone and is not saved or uploaded.

Who is it for?

Anyone who understands English but wants to speak it with more confidence.
