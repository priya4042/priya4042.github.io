What it does

MCA Prep Hub lays out every subject and topic for MCA entrance exams with a priority tag, tells you exactly where to prepare each one - books, YouTube and practice sites - and lets you test yourself as you go.

Features

  • 9 subjects with every topic tagged by priority
  • About 690 practice questions with simple answers
  • Test Me quiz to check yourself
  • A study plan to follow
  • Save questions and track your progress
  • Works in any browser, free
